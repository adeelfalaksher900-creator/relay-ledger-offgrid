## Grounding

Ink & Switch, local-first software: https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/local-first/ . The useful product principle is working on owned local data while enabling later collaboration. It is research context, not evidence of demand for this specific prototype.

Automerge conflicts: https://automerge.org/docs/reference/documents/conflicts/ . Concurrent property assignments have competing values; a deterministic library winner alone does not settle a human decision. Relay Ledger uses a small original causal field-register model and shows different maximal values explicitly. Automerge is a comparison reference, not a shipped dependency.

Browser security: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Crypto_API ; https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AesGcmParams ; https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SubtleCrypto/deriveKey ; https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/ServiceWorker . These APIs require a secure context. Unique IVs, correct derivation parameters and offline cache behavior are engineering requirements, not slogans.

Existing animated optical transfer: https://github.com/Hitesh-Sisara/qr-code-file-transfer and https://github.com/zhulin025/qrrec-optical-transfer . Screen-to-camera transfer already exists and sophisticated implementations use fountain codes. Relay Ledger does not claim novel transport or their throughput; its scope is a deliberately small, understandable team handoff and conflict decision workflow, with file fallback.

## Product hypothesis

A student club or volunteer crew may need small shared task lists when connectivity fails. The hypothesized value is continuity with visible disagreement. No interviews, measured adoption, outcomes, physical-camera throughput or commercial-market size have been fabricated. Next validation: paired-device trials, lost-frame observation and small-group usability sessions with consent.
